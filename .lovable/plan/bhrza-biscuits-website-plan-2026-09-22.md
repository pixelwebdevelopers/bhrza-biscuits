# BHRZA Biscuits Website Plan

## Goal
Build a polished multi-page website for BHRZA Biscuits that immediately communicates Pakistani biscuit manufacturing, family tea-time moments, and the Nowshera-based company identity. The uploaded BHRZA logo and favicon will be used as official brand assets.

## Pages and navigation
- **Home** — cinematic biscuit opening, brand story, demo product showcase, BHRZA values, Pakistani family tea-time feature, NKT Food's introduction, verified quality symbols, chai feature, Facebook feature, and contact call-to-action.
- **About** — BHRZA introduction, NKT Food's Pvt Ltd., Nowshera location, replaceable brand philosophy, quality-focused messaging, manufacturing imagery, and Pakistani lifestyle imagery.
- **Products** — premium catalogue with four clearly identified demo products and editable placeholders for pack size, ingredients, and nutrition.
- **Quality** — focused explanation of the supplied packaging concepts: Halal, Pakistan Standards / PSQCA, recyclable packaging, and heat/sunlight protection in English and Urdu.
- **Contact** — verified address and email, Facebook link, contact form, email action, and a clearly labelled location placeholder.

## Visual direction
- Warm cream and white editorial layouts with strategic BHRZA red, coral accents, charcoal typography, and biscuit-gold details.
- Bold Manrope typography, strong logo presence, rounded product cards, restrained shadows, and generous spacing.
- Sticky navigation that subtly changes after scrolling, compact mobile navigation, measured reveal motion, image hover scale, and reduced-motion support.
- Shared header and footer across all pages, with route-specific page titles and social descriptions.

## Custom imagery
Generate a cohesive commercial-photography set specifically for BHRZA:
- Premium hero biscuits in a Pakistani-inspired tea setting.
- Editorial biscuit close-up and flat-lay scenes.
- Original red BHRZA-inspired **demo** package concepts that do not imitate an unprovided pack design.
- Authentic Pakistani family tea-time scenes in a modern home.
- Chai and biscuit editorial photography.
- Pakistani food-manufacturing environment and workers.

Images with people will depict Pakistani people naturally and avoid stereotypical styling. Generated product visuals will be presented as temporary concepts, not verified products.

## Content integrity
- Use only the supplied company name, location, email, website, Facebook handle, and packaging symbols as facts.
- Do not introduce unsupported history, metrics, capabilities, ingredients, nutrition, pack weights, partnerships, awards, or additional certifications.
- Mark temporary product names and missing catalogue fields as demo/placeholders so they can be replaced easily.
- Contact form will validate entries in the browser and prepare an email to the verified company address; no database or invented phone number will be added.

## Brand assets and finish
- Host the supplied logo as a website asset and place it prominently in navigation, footer, and key brand moments.
- Replace the default favicon with the supplied favicon.
- Build reusable page sections and product data so future product details are easy to swap.
- Check desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, form validation, visual spacing, image loading, and page metadata before completion.
